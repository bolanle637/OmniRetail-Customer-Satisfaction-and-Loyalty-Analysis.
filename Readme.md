# OMNIRETAIL: CUSTOMER SATISFACTION & LOYALTY ANALYSIS

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing customer satisfaction and loyalty for OmniRetail, using Microsoft Excel to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights.

The analysis explores customer satisfaction levels, loyalty patterns, purchasing behaviour, customer support experiences, geographical differences and demographic preferences.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed using PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI cards and slicers to help management understand customer behaviour, identify areas requiring improvement and make data-driven business decisions.

## Business Problem

OmniRetail faces several customer-related business challenges that may affect customer retention, purchasing behaviour and long-term business growth.

The project investigates nine key business questions:

1. Low overall customer satisfaction.
2. Low customer loyalty and customer retention.
3. Differences in customer satisfaction across geographical locations.
4. Customer support effectiveness and its impact on satisfaction.
5. The relationship between Price, Product Variety and customer loyalty.
6. The relationship between purchasing history and customer satisfaction.
7. Geographical differences in customer loyalty and dissatisfaction.
8. The relationship between customer satisfaction and loyalty levels.
9. Differences in customer preferences across demographic groups.

## Key Findings and Insights

### 1. Customer Satisfaction

The overall average satisfaction score is 5.35 out of 10. Ease of Use (4.07), Features (4.08) and Price (4.40) record the lowest satisfaction scores, indicating areas requiring management attention.

### 2. Customer Loyalty

Only 30.83% of customers fall within the High loyalty category, while 37.50% fall within Low loyalty. This highlights the importance of strengthening customer retention strategies.

### 3. Geographical Performance

Chicago records the highest average customer satisfaction at 6.36, while San Antonio records the lowest at 3.50. This indicates differences in customer experiences across geographical locations.

### 4. Customer Support

Approximately 46.67% of customers contacted customer support. The average satisfaction scores for customers who contacted support and those who did not are very similar, at 5.36 and 5.34 respectively.

### 5. Purchasing Behaviour

Customers with recorded purchase history have an average satisfaction score of 5.62, compared with 4.98 for customers without recorded purchase history.

### 6. Satisfaction and Loyalty

Low loyalty customers recorded an average satisfaction score of 5.84, compared with 5.65 for High loyalty customers and 4.47 for Medium loyalty customers. This highlights the need to investigate the relationship between satisfaction and continued customer loyalty.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

- Improve product usability and functionality.
- Review pricing strategies to strengthen perceived customer value.
- Introduce customer loyalty rewards and retention programmes.
- Investigate customer dissatisfaction in underperforming locations.
- Improve customer support accessibility and complaint resolution.
- Develop targeted marketing strategies for different customer segments.
- Monitor purchasing behaviour and customer satisfaction regularly.
